How weights work

Your score is first converted to a percentage. The weight then sets how much that result counts toward your subject average.

For example, a test with weight 2 counts twice as much as a quiz with weight 1, regardless of the number of points.

Use your school’s published weights. If every result counts equally, use 1 for each assessment.
